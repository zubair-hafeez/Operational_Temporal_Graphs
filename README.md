# Operational Temporal Graphs

Research on when relations help collective forecasting for active cohorts in operational histories.

- [Current paper](paper_revision/main.pdf) and [LaTeX source](paper_revision/main.tex).
- [Current study review](extension/REVIEW.txt), [reproduction guide](extension/REPRODUCE.txt), and [confirmation protocol](extension/CONFIRMATION_PROTOCOL.json).
- [Publication audit](reports/extension_20261021/publication_audit.json) and [manuscript tables and source values](paper_revision/generated/).
- Implementation: `otg/`; current experiments: `extension/`; supporting scripts: `scripts/` and `revision/`; tests: `tests/`.

The retained manuscript is *When Do Relations Help Collective Forecasts?* It studies Borg, Azure V2, BPI 2017/2012 and two incident-log sources. Results are conditional on the tested sources and controls; the study does not establish a universal graph-model advantage.

## Public contents

This repository contains a curated selection of essential files. Raw datasets, downloaded literature PDFs, model checkpoints, caches, routine logs, temporary files, worker staging and redundant exports are omitted. The complete original project remains local. No full-backup Release is retained.

The [publication manifest](PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json) records the included source files and their hashes. Existing research results and documents were copied without rerunning experiments or independently revalidating their scientific claims. Historical documents may refer to files that are available only in the local project.

Some saved-output audits and rebuild commands require omitted local inputs. Third-party material retains its original terms; no new blanket license is granted.
