Scientific review: six-source extension, 22 October 2026
======================================================
The paper is a venue-neutral empirical benchmark, not a new universal graph
architecture. No external submission or public release has occurred. This
review is an internal critical check, not independent peer review.

1. More data, sampled first
   Completed Google2019 shard audit, Azure prefix then complete-subscription
   sampling, BPI2014 changes audit and incident replay, UCI incident replay,
   CitiBike and two airline geographies. Promoted Azure, UCI and BPI2014 to
   three-seed development and 28-origin frozen confirmation each. Total paper:
   six scored sources and 201 origins; only 84 belong to this new confirmation.
   Repeated entities and seeds are not independent organizational replications.

2. Temporal techniques and graph benefits
   Compared temporal attention, typed message passing, temporal-history HGB,
   node MLP and global-set controls. A graph can improve individual marginals;
   a relation-specific copula can improve collective uncertainty while holding
   those marginals fixed. These are distinct claims. Neither is universally
   successful across sources. The manuscript now explains this distinction.

3. Stronger graph baseline
   Three-seed fits, feature-matched temporal HGB, same-budget shuffled graph,
   single-relation graphs, node-only/set controls. Also a 176-feature matched
   Borg pilot: graph models lose to strong tree summaries on validation.
   Fixed training budgets and independent target adaptations are disclosed.

4. Relationship types
   Original job/machine removals retained. New handler/team, deployment/
   subscription, station, airport/carrier/destination arms fitted separately.
   UCI combined typed Gaussian improves global CRPS 3.3%; typed Student-t 3.6%.
   Azure development-selected subscription-only improves global CRPS 4.7%.
   Its combined coordinate fit collapses to global: bounded optimization, not
   proof of no relation signal. BPI2014 combined typed gain is only 0.2%.
   Neural and coupling shuffles are different controls; missing-mask and
   risk-stratification differences are disclosed.

5. Modern and joint baselines
   Temporal attention and graph heads; Gaussian and matched global/typed
   Student-t; direct concentration-only and bias-DM; exact beta-binomial
   count scores; residual trajectories; prior reconciliation comparisons.
   Bounded adapted upstream TACTiS copula does not improve matched subsets.
   This is not a claim against full TGN/TGAT/DyGFormer/TACTiS-2 systems.
   UCI bias-DM and typed Student-t are effectively tied on count CRPS.
   BPI2014 bias correction wins development but loses confirmation. Negative
   findings are retained instead of searching the final period for a winner.

6. Presentation
   New title and abstract; clear six-source evidence stages; generated main
   count/marginal tables; paired-effect figure; full relation weights and
   joint diagnostics in appendix. Latest paper only, no duplicate archives.
   Every result must be traceable to saved outputs and current generation code.

Remaining publication risks
---------------------------
The contribution is evaluation/empirical evidence, not a novel statistical
model. A selective general-method conference may require substantially more
methodological novelty; acceptance cannot be guaranteed by additional runs.
Two compute providers broaden scope but Azure/Borg have seven final days each.
Incident archives may be completion-conditioned and do not prove live-population
availability. Naive incident timestamps use a UTC parsing convention, not a
verified operational timezone or daylight-saving reconstruction.
No arrivals, repeated transitions, deployment latency or live
staffing/resource savings are modeled. Strong direct models often match or beat
coherent entity scenarios. A narrow typed win is not universal graph necessity.
Primary intervals are descriptive and not multiplicity-adjusted. Azure's
whole-trace weekly bootstrap is degenerate. Supplementary selected-model
intervals were added after confirmation and are marked as such. No final
period may be reused for tuning and then called fresh validation.

For an eventual submission, the researcher must approve target semantics,
authors, affiliations, funding/conflicts, third-party terms and AI-assistance
disclosure, and choose a venue and permanent artifact location. The user chose
to keep this manuscript venue-neutral. Do not fabricate those details or
submit/upload on their behalf. Broader temporal replication or a full published
model adaptation would be future research with a new protocol and holdout,
not a cosmetic claim that this paper already proves it.
