\section{Related work and scope}
\paragraph{Temporal prediction and generation.}
GraphMixer demonstrates the strength of simple temporal encoders for link
prediction \citep{cong2023}. We use an independently implemented Mixer-style
survival adaptation, alongside recurrent and tabular controls, rather than
claiming to reproduce its original link-prediction protocol. CEP3 forecasts
community events and their times with a graph point process \citep{wang2022};
DG-Gen autoregressively generates continuous-time dynamic graphs
\citep{hosseini2025}. These are relevant generative approaches, but their
published tasks do not by themselves establish reliable distributions of
current-cohort first exits. We do not report numerical superiority over these
systems: a fair comparison would require explicit adaptations of outcome
semantics, state initialization, horizon stopping, and candidate populations.

\paragraph{Joint probabilistic forecasting.}
TACTiS and TACTiS-2 learn copula-based multivariate predictive distributions and
support irregular or unaligned time series \citep{drouin2022,ashok2024}.
Separating fitted marginals from a spatial Gaussian copula is also established,
including in neural rainfall downscaling \citep{huk2025}. Our reference models
use more restrictive dependence families, including the classical
Marshall--Olkin shock construction \citep{marshall1967}. The practical
distinction is the use of current typed incidence to share random factors among
changing entity populations, with identical individual forecasts across
controls. We claim neither a universal modeling advantage over attentional
copulas nor a new marginal--dependence decomposition.

\paragraph{Operational simulation and uncertainty.}
RIMS integrates learned predictions with business process simulation and
runtime intercase information \citep{meneghello2025}. AgentSimulator models
resource behavior and interactions \citep{kirchdorfer2025}. Closely related
work reconstructs current process states and estimates state-conditional
forecast-error intervals, including on BPI 2017 \citep{avramenko2026}.
Thus, neither current-state initialization nor uncertainty estimation for
operational forecasts is an unaddressed problem. Our study instead isolates
joint outcome dependence under fixed individual CDFs, tests relation incidence,
and compares multiple group projections and decision losses across a cluster
and a workflow. The historical-error baseline reflects the relevance of direct
aggregate uncertainty estimation; it is not a reproduction of the complete
simulation system in \citet{avramenko2026}.

Resource-trace generation also has a distinct operational objective. Mimesys
synthesizes executable workloads to reproduce resource contention
\citep{kim2026}. Our sampled first-exit bins cannot substitute for executable
workloads or full process simulations. They target the uncertainty of the
observed cohort under the logged operating regime.
