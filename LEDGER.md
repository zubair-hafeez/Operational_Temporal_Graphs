## 2026-10-14 Full-cohort scenario validation and stronger temporal controls

All nine initial HGB fits and six Borg recent-history HGB fits completed. Audited raw sorted CDFs, independently Platt-calibrated sorted CDFs, and shared positive Platt calibration with population/equal-cutoff weighting. The declared development selection score is equal-cutoff integrated Brier over four horizons. History features count only past task/global/job/machine events in 1/5/15/60-minute windows. More features do not guarantee better marginals: best recent-history pending IBrier .065995 versus original global .064148. Treat history and original versions as explicit alternatives; do not call history uniformly stronger in accuracy.

Prepared cross-horizon joint data: Borg 628,081 rows, 720,000 training and 240,000 validation pairs; BPI 141,839 rows, 150,000/51,000 pairs. Pair sampling is outcome-independent, mixes uniform node/group and group-size-weighted pairs, includes cross-state pairs, and masks unknown relations. Dependence models share global and typed relation randomness across the complete cohort, including streamed chunks. Each entity receives one latent uniform across all horizons. Fixed a zero-weight exponential-race numerical edge case. Twenty-nine local tests now pass, including explicit-vs-streamed projected count scores, perfect deterministic trajectories, history leakage/ties, and neural CDF/backpropagation.

Full development seed0 results (512 draws, equal cutoff/projection/horizon/group weights): Gaussian Borg independent .023049, global .018615, static .018238, context .018227; common-shock Borg independent .023056, global .019055, static .018243, context .018259. Gaussian BPI independent .043650, global .038720, static .039012, context .039699. Common-shock BPI independent .043567, global .041341, static .041612, context .041360. Operational quantities now include normalized count CRPS, 90% interval coverage/width/score, multihorizon energy score, fraction of groups with >=20% cohort exits, and asymmetric reserve loss at ratios 3/9. Reserve loss concerns only unexited members of the currently observed cohort. It is a decision proxy, not a causal policy evaluation or currency savings; no future arrivals or exclusive handler capacity is inferred. BPI Gaussian global improves population coverage from .235 to .956 but this may compensate common misspecification as well as actual dependence.
